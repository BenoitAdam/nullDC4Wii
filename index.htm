NullDC4Wii
Dreamcast on your Wii

An open-source Dreamcast emulator built for Nintendo Wii. Boot up the GD-ROM classics again — on hardware that's been sitting in a drawer since 2012.

Get the latest release

Every build is published on GitHub Releases — grab the newest version, drop it on your SD card, and you're back on the Dreamcast.

Check what runs

03 — Donations

Keep the disc spinning

NullDC4Wii is built and maintained by volunteers developers, on nights and weekends. Every donation means "thank you for the already accomplished job", and also goes straight back into debugging time, hardware for testing, and the occasional game that just refuses to boot right.
